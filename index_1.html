07

Daily approach strategy

The niche is finite — the goal is staying in front of the same ~170K–340K people consistently, without wearing them out, while building a warmer layer inside it.

Always-on spend

Keep one ad set live at all times per campaign — every restart re-enters the learning phase and resets found efficiency.

Retargeting layers

Re-engage WhatsApp openers who went quiet and page/profile engagers — both cheaper than fresh cold targeting.

Lookalike audience

Once 100+ real messages/leads accumulate, build a 1–3% lookalike — layered with the same Karachi-only location setting.

Fatigue protection

Rotate creative every 10–14 days on a staggered schedule; watch Frequency and swap before CTR actually drops.

08

Recommended campaign structure & rollout

Consolidating toward fewer, better-funded campaigns — 1–2 well-funded ad sets consistently outperform several thin ones.
WEEKS 1–2 · TEST

Collect learning-phase data

  • Run all 4 campaigns at starting budgets, unchanged
  • Goal: ~50 events/ad set/week
  • No optimization decisions yet
WEEKS 3–4 · OPTIMIZE

Cut, keep, reallocate

  • Pause ad sets above cost benchmarks
  • Shift budget to cheapest-per-result
  • Compare video vs. static formats
MONTH 2+ · SCALE

Grow what's working

  • +20–30% budget increments only
  • Introduce the lookalike audience
  • Rotate service-line messaging weekly
